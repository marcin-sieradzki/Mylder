# Stop panel

A panel for one stop: its name, its lines as badges, "At the platforms now" with the trips standing at its platforms (on a phone, a row of cards that scrolls sideways), and below them two tabs, Departures and Arrivals, each listing the next 30 minutes with a countdown and its data state, Metro rows as intervals, its messages and a source line. It is kept in the address as `?stop=<id>`, and its Arrivals tab as `&board=arrivals`.

## Sub-features

- `stop-open` opens from a stop on the map, a disruption's ringed stop, or a shared link; on a phone, in front of the Overview.
- `stop-list` lists departures with countdowns; the Metro as "every N min".
- `stop-first-paint` opened by link, shows each countdown as "—" and no "may be out of date" until the page's first snapshot gives it the server's clock, never "Now" on every row.
- `stop-standing` lists the trips standing at the stop's platforms above the departures, 4 of them until "Show all N" shows the rest, each opening its vehicle, or says none stands.
- `stop-standing-phone` shows the same trips on a phone as one row of cards that scrolls sideways above the tabs, every one of them with no "Show all".
- `stop-arrivals` switches to the Arrivals tab, which lists the trips arriving, each from where it comes, the Metro as an interval, or says none arrives; the address keeps the tab. A stop with a recorded arrival board answers from it, with its tracks and "Rejseplanen · arrivals"; the rest from the timetable.
- `stop-language` keeps the panel open across the language switch.
- `stop-missing` opens a not-found panel for an unknown stop.
- `stop-timetable` on the timetable source lists each departure and arrival at its planned time with the delay unknown, the Metro as its interval, and names the timetable as the source: "Timetable · 08:14:05".

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Tap a stop's marker on the map. On a phone the stop's sheet opens in front of the Overview's, link or tap alike, with the Overview left open behind it.
- Tap a ringed stop after Show on map on a disruption.
- Open a shared link with `?stop=<id>`, or `?stop=<id>&board=arrivals` for its Arrivals.
- Choose the Arrivals tab in an open stop's panel, by a tap or the arrow keys and Enter.

## Gotchas

- Before the page's clock settles, every countdown reads `—`, the standing trips' too, while each row's clock time already shows; read by the device's clock, every row would read `Now` and a fresh board's source line `may be out of date`. `src/screens/StopPanel.test.tsx` covers it with the device a day ahead.
- A stop marker sits under passing dots at times; tap again inside `toPass()` until the stop's panel, not a vehicle's, opens.
- Every stop but København Syd St. answers from the timetable on the recording, on both tabs, its source line "From the timetable" and its rows without a track; only København Syd St. has recorded boards, a departure board and an arrival board, which answer it whenever it is opened, so its trains show a track (8, made up by the simulation, one per platform). The 991 standing at Refshaleøen and Orientkaj St. is a bus with no board and shows no track or stand.
- A standing trip's dot sits on its stop's marker, or within a tap of it at zoom 15, and a dot wins over a stop: hide its mode with the filter for the tap and show it again once the panel is open, or zoom in until the dot is more than 24 px off the marker.
- On a phone the stop's sheet opens at its peek, in front of the Overview's, and the tabs show at the peek: it rises as far as the header, the standing row and the whole `Departures and arrivals` tablist need, never less than Perron's `size.sheet-peek`, so the tablist ends at the screen's foot, or higher with room under it where the tabs end above that token's height; no drag or scroll is needed. It rises again when a standing trip comes in, by a filter or the next minute's answer, and settles lower when the row empties, but never while a finger drags the sheet: the new peek waits for the drag's end. The map's foot, the corner credit with it, stands on whichever peek the stop's sheet has, so the credit stays whole above it. Check the tabs with `toBeInViewport()`, not a click, which scrolls the body. Dragging its header up raises it full height, under the top bar and the recorded-data notice, which then cover the row's top.
- On the `weekday` recording no stop has more than one non-Metro trip at its platforms at once, at any minute of the day, so "Show all N" (shown past four standing) and a phone's row of more than one card are driven on the `crowded` recording, whose timetable is made by hand from the fixture's trains (`scripts/timetable/fixtures/gtfs-crowded`). Its trains run to their timetable, so each standing row's delay reads `On time`, and its state word is `Estimated` in the run's first minute and `Live` or `Estimated` after; it holds five minutes, after which the trains have left København H, so drive "Show all" within its first three.
- The `crowded` recording's own timetable has only those six trains' stops, and no bus or Metro: drive its run for the standing list alone, and every other recipe on `weekday`.
- Arrivals ask Rejseplanen's arrival board for every stop but a Metro-only one. On the recording only København Syd St.'s is kept; any other stop's call fails as an unknown call does, so its Arrivals come from the timetable plus the snapshot's delays, their source line `Timetable and positions <time>`, no row with a track, and the note "Countdowns use the timetable, moved by the delay…". That failed call still counts in the About dialog's "Calls this month".
- No recording shows a cancelled arrival: the weekday's 1 % of cancelled trips misses København Syd St.'s board. `src/screens/StopPanel.test.tsx` covers the row marked Cancelled with its countdown struck, and `src/tracker/stop-board.test.ts` the board's cancelled arrival kept.
- "Now" under 45 s on an arrival follows the departures' rule but is hard to time on a recording: Nørreport St.'s E from Holte reads it only from about 07:27:15 to 07:29, as its expected arrival moves between 07:28 and 07:29 with each minute's positions. `src/screens/StopPanel.test.tsx` covers it.
- A Metro stop's arrivals interval lasts only while a planned arrival is ahead in the window: Bella Center St.'s last is at 07:26, Vestamager St.'s at 07:30; after that their Arrivals say none arrives. Vestamager St. has arrivals but no departures interval, since M1 ends there and its trips that leave it left before 07:20.
- On the timetable source a row's countdown keeps Perron's `~` before it (`~6 min`), as every Timetable only row does; the clock time beside it has none, which is what says no expected time is shown.
- A standing trip stands only for minutes: Refshaleøen's bus leaves about 07:24 on the recording's clock, and Orientkaj St.'s stands from 07:25 until 07:32. The Metro is never listed.
