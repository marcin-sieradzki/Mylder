# Recordings

Rejseplanen answers the tracker replays with `MYLDER_SOURCE=recording:<name>`, one folder each: the answers per service in call order, a departure or arrival board's in a folder for the stops it was asked for (`multiDepartureBoard/8600783/`, `multiArrivalBoard/8600783/`), the `timetable/` they belong to, `clock.json` with the moment they start, `snapshots/` with the tracker's snapshot of each minute, which screen tests use, and `answers/` with the route answers they use beside it: the disruption list of each minute in `answers/disruptions/` and the stop, arrivals and trip answers a plan saves.

Until the key arrives, every recording is frozen from the simulation by `npm run recordings`, from the plans in `src/tracker/recording/plans.ts`, each on the fixture timetable its plan names in `scripts/timetable/fixtures/`; edit the plans and the fixtures, not the files. `docs/testing-seams.md` describes the format and the hand-made cases.

`timetable-source/` and `timetable-ending/` are no recordings: each is the tracker's run on the timetable source (`MYLDER_SOURCE=timetable`), which makes no call, saved by the same script from its plan, `ON_TIMETABLE` for screen tests and `ENDING_TIMETABLE` for the timetable's last week. Each holds the `timetable/`, `snapshots/` and `answers/` a recording does, and its start in `clock.json`, which `MYLDER_TIMETABLE_FROM=<folder>` starts the timetable source at, but no API answers, so `MYLDER_SOURCE=recording:<folder>` has nothing to replay.

## Aircraft

A recording may hold the aircraft feed's answers too, replayed with `MYLDER_AIRCRAFT_SOURCE=recording:<name>` (`docs/aircraft.md`): `aircraft/`, one answer per 10 s call in call order, which `npm run recordings` keeps as it is, and `answers/aircraft/`, the aircraft snapshot of each call, which it saves from them. An `aircraft/` folder may hold its own `clock.json` when its answers were recorded on another day than the rest. `weekday/aircraft/` is three minutes of real adsb.lol answers over Copenhagen, recorded on 26 October 2026 from 19:53:13 Copenhagen time, one every 10 s, each cut to the aircraft round the transit box and three outside it, with every other field kept (`docs/aircraft.md`, "Recordings"). The demo bundles whatever `weekday/aircraft/` holds and replays it in a loop (`docs/aircraft.md`, "The demo"). They are adsb.lol's data, © adsb.lol contributors under the [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), and stay open here under the same licence.

## SIRI-ET

`siri-et/` is no Rejseplanen API recording either: it is 56 minutes of Rejseplanen's real SIRI-ET messages from Dataudveksleren, the four datasets that cover Copenhagen, on Saturday 31 October 2026, cut to the journeys that call round the transit box, beside its service day's timetable and a `clock.json`. Its own `README.md` says how it was recorded and cut, and what it holds. The SIRI-ET reader's tests (`src/tracker/siri-et/`) replay a sample of it, eight journeys cut by `scripts/siri-et/sample-siri-et.mjs`, and `scripts/siri-et/replay-siri-et.ts` replays the whole of it for the update table's figures in `docs/live-data.md`.
