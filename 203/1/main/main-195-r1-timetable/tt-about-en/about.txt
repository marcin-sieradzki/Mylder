A live map of Copenhagen's buses, S-tog, Metro and trains, honest about how fresh each position is.

Mylder shows the data as Rejseplanen sends it, in its own words and language, and marks every figure it works out itself.

Mylder is non-commercial: no advertising, no paid tier, and no sale of the data or of anything built on it.

DATA AND CREDITS

Realtime data and the timetable: Rejseplanen, under CC BY 4.0. Timetable of 1 November.

Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors under the ODbL, © OpenMapTiles, OpenFreeMap.

Building heights come from OpenStreetMap and are approximate.

Aircraft: © adsb.lol contributors under the ODbL.

The planes are recorded adsb.lol data, open under the same licence in Mylder's repository.

The map's style is a fork of OpenFreeMap's Positron, itself from OpenMapTiles' Positron GL Style: see its licence.

DATA STATES
Live

The vehicle itself reported this position within about the last two polls.

Ageing

The vehicle's last report is older than that but under 5 minutes; the dot moves on from it along the line.

Estimated

No recent report, but Rejseplanen still has the trip: a position it computed, or expected times.

Timetable only

No realtime for this trip today, so it is placed from the timetable. Every Metro train is.

No signal

The trip had realtime, and it stopped 5 minutes ago or more; the dot moves on the timetable and its last delay.

KEYS

+ and − zoom the map.

← → ↑ ↓ move it.

Shift + ← and → turn the map.

Shift + ↑ and ↓ tilt it.

Esc closes the vehicle card or panel.

The pause button, beside the zoom buttons, stops the vehicles moving, and the play button starts them again.
